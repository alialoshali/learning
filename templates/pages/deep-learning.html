What this course covers

The course follows the main families of machine learning. Use the index on the right to jump to any topic, or follow the order below.

TopicWhat you learn
Machine learningWhat it means to learn from data instead of writing rules.
Supervised learningLearning from examples that have known answers.
Linear regressionFit a line, measure error, improve it with gradient descent.
Logistic regressionPredict categories and probabilities.
Deep learningNeural networks and transformers.
Unsupervised learningFind structure in data without answers: clustering, PCA.
Reinforcement learningLearn by trial and reward.

How each lesson works

  • Idea. The problem and the model in plain words.
  • Equation. Every formula lists its symbols and where it comes from.
  • Experiment. Sliders and buttons to see the maths move.
  • Transformer link. How the same idea appears in modern language models.
Where to startNew to the subject? Begin with linear regression. Every later topic reuses its three steps: model, loss, update.
